## Design System — The Financial Architect
- Deep teal professional fintech theme
- No 1px borders — use ghost borders or ambient shadows
- Surface hierarchy through elevation, not borders
- No pure black (#000) or pure white (#fff)
- Signature gradient for primary CTAs
- Glassmorphism for navigation elements
- Editorial-grade typography
- Refer to `docs/designplan.md` for full design specifications
